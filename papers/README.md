# Papers

The framework is written up in five papers, isolated by tier, so that adverse review of one layer does not
invalidate the others. This is the five-paper publication strategy referred to in [../THEORY.md](../THEORY.md).

| Paper | Scope | Tier | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[A](paper_A_late_time/)** | Late-time framework and its registered tests | T1 + T2 | Draft; carries flags for open problems 1, 2 and 8 ([../OPEN_PROBLEMS.md](../OPEN_PROBLEMS.md)) |
| **[B](paper_B_rg_mechanism/)** | Renormalization-group mechanism for G(σ) | T3 | Draft of an obstructed module; kept as the record of why it is obstructed |
| **[C](paper_C_extensions/)** | Speculative extension modules | T4 | Outline |
| **[D](paper_D_methodology/)** | The pre-registration and self-verification method | method | Draft plus a JOSS-format paper ([paper_D_methodology/joss/paper.md](paper_D_methodology/joss/paper.md)); independent of the physics outcome |
| **[E](paper_E_self_falsification/)** | Case report: the audit that falsified its operators' own central claim | method | Draft |

A reviewer can reject Paper C without affecting Paper A, reject Paper B's mechanism without affecting Paper A's
tests, and cite Papers D and E without endorsing any physical claim.

Recommended submission order: D (method, lowest review risk), E (case report), then A; B and C only if their
modules revive.
